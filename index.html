My story

How I got from the plan to the packet

Every network I worked around made me want to understand it more deeply. This is how that curiosity became a career.

2012 – 2017

Help desk and field operations in Bogotá

Open English · Outsourcing SAS for Claro

I started in technical support, troubleshooting for students on an online learning platform. Later I led a five-person team coordinating Claro’s home-service field operations: assigning work, tracking technicians and reporting results.

2021 – 2026

Inside Cisco, where it clicked

Project Specialist, then Inventory Project Manager

Planning hardware for Duke Energy, I saw how a set of well-chosen routers, switches and fiber links connects the substations that deliver electricity to millions of customers. I wanted to understand every connection and every packet, not just the spreadsheet.

2026

From curiosity to certification

Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA 200-301)

I studied on my own and passed the CCNA in November 2026: switching, routing, wireless, security and automation, from electrical signals on the wire to the life cycle of a packet.

Next

Building, documenting, growing

Supermarkets, banks, utilities: everything runs on a network. I want to be one of the people who keeps it running. I’m building documented labs, learning Linux and Python, and working toward the CCNP.

Every time I go deeper into networking, I find more to discover. That is what keeps me moving forward.
